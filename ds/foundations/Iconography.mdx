# Iconography

24px icons on a 24px grid, 1.6px stroke, round caps and joins (Figma `ico/*`). Colour is baked per use: `#111117` on light, white on dark, `#1A3946` for link arrows. Arrows in buttons and links are always `ico/arrow/right` (24px). The 20px variant is only used in mega-menu column heads.

One exception: `ico/ai` (32px, Figma 1227:27470) is the multi-colour AI mark, a four-point star filled with blurred red, blue, green and yellow fields. It lives only in the Insights badge (white 54px circle, `stroke/primary`).

Exported from Figma into `assets/v2/ico` and `assets/v2/social`.
