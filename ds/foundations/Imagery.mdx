# Imagery

**Subjects:** the people and vehicles Foley customers manage. Trucks, ambulances, construction and agricultural machinery, school buses, fleet managers at work, the product on real screens. Daylight or golden hour, saturated but natural colour. No staged handshakes, no abstract stock.
